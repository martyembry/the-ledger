The Ledger

The Ledger is a growing record of Black children and young adults who died under circumstances that were never fully explained. It started with a handful of documented cases and keeps expanding, one verified entry at a time, with particular attention to the deaths of Black children and young people that got little scrutiny or coverage when they happened.

Every case here carries at least two independent, named sources before it's added. A status of unresolved means official findings and family accounts still conflict, not that guilt or innocence has been decided one way or the other. Each entry gets checked against that standard before it goes up, and stays open to correction as more reporting comes out.
